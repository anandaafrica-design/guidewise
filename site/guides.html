Questions

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Install it on your home screen and your tours, plans, guests and documents open anywhere. Only AI import, weather and backup need a connection.

iPhone or Android?

Both, plus computers. It installs from the browser in a few taps; no app store needed. How to install

What about my guests' privacy?

Guest details stay on your phone. Backups are encrypted on your phone first, so we cannot read them. AI import cleans spreadsheets on your phone before sending and asks your agreement every time. Privacy notice

Can I cancel?

Any time, in the app. Plus keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, and everything on your phone stays free. Terms and pricing

Who makes Guidewise?

Guidewise is made in Cape Town by a working tour director, for guides everywhere. Questions: guidewise.app@gmail.com
