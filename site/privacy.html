Guidewise privacy notice

Version popia-v2 · AI import ai-v1 · 3 November 2026

Who we are

Guidewise is a tour preparation app run by Ananda van der Merwe, Cape Town, South Africa. Contact for privacy questions and requests: guidewise.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

By default everything you enter (tour plan, guests, checklists, notes, documents) is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us unless you switch on backup.

If you switch on backup

  • Your email address is used to sign you in and to identify your backup.
  • Your tour data, including guest names, rooms, birthdays, dietary needs and notes, is encrypted on your phone before upload. Only your passphrase or your one-time recovery code can unlock it. We store only the encrypted copy and cannot read it.
  • A consent record: the date you agreed to this notice.
  • Your documents (vouchers, PDFs, photos), if you leave "Back up documents too" on: each file is encrypted on your phone with the same key before upload. File names and details are stored only inside your encrypted backup. You can switch this off, and deleting a document, your backup or your account deletes the cloud copy.

If you subscribe to Guidewise Plus

Card payments are processed by Paystack (Paystack Payments South Africa). Paystack receives your email address and card details; we never see your card number. We store your plan, its status, renewal date and Paystack's customer and subscription references, so the app knows whether backup is included. Paystack keeps payment records as financial law requires. See the terms and pricing.

Weather

To show a forecast and sun times, the app sends only the town or area you enter for a day and its date to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. For public holidays it sends only the country and year to Nager.Date. No names or guest details are sent.

If you use AI import (Plus)

  • You choose the documents and agree each time before anything is sent.
  • On your phone first: spreadsheets lose passport, visa, flight, phone, email, address and insurance columns, and dates of birth are cut to day and month. Emails and phone numbers are removed from text files. PDFs and photos are sent as they are.
  • The documents pass through our server (Supabase) to Anthropic PBC in the United States, which reads them with its Claude AI and returns a draft tour. This is a transfer outside South Africa, which you agree to under section 72 of POPIA. Anthropic acts as our operator: it does not use the documents to train its models and deletes them within 30 days, unless they are flagged for a safety review.
  • We do not store the documents or the draft. We keep only a usage record: date, number of files and amount of text processed, to apply the monthly limit and our costs.
  • The draft tour is saved on your phone only, like any other tour.
  • Only send documents you are allowed to share. Many operators have rules on guest data; check yours.

If you translate a briefing (Plus)

When you tap Translate, the briefing text (meeting time, weather, dress and the day's stops) goes through our server to Anthropic in the United States to be translated, on the same terms as AI import above. The briefing never contains guest names, birthdays or dietary needs. We keep only a usage count for the monthly limit.

Why, and on what basis

To give you a backup and let you restore your tour on another device, and to run your subscription. For the backup and AI import we rely on your consent; for billing, on our agreement with you. Dietary and health notes about guests are special personal information under POPIA. As the guide, you are responsible for having your guests' agreement to record them.

Where it is stored

Backups are held by Supabase, our service provider, in London, United Kingdom. This is a transfer outside South Africa, which you agree to under section 72 of POPIA. The UK has data protection law comparable to POPIA.

How long

Until you delete your backup or your account in the app. Deleting your account removes your email, backup, consent record and subscription details from our systems, and cancels any Plus subscription.

Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent at any time (delete your backup in the app), and object to processing. You may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.
