New accounts get a 30-day free trial of Plus. No card is needed for the trial, and it does not turn into a paid plan by itself.

Paying and renewing

Payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card number. Plus renews automatically each month or year on the same card until you cancel.

If a renewal fails, Plus keeps working for 7 days while you update your card.

Cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel any time in the app: Back up, then Cancel subscription. You will not be charged again, and Plus keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Changed your mind? Tap Keep my subscription before then.
  • You may cancel within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund (Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, section 44). Email us to arrange it.
  • After that, payments are not refunded for part periods.
  • When Plus ends, backups pause. Your backup stays available for you to restore or delete, and everything on your device keeps working.

Your guests' information

You decide what guest information you record and are responsible for having your guests' agreement under POPIA. Backups are encrypted on your device; we cannot read them. AI import sends the documents you choose to our AI provider; only send documents you are allowed to share. See the privacy notice.

AI import makes a draft. It can misread or miss things, so check the tour against your documents before relying on it.

Availability and liability

We work to keep Guidewise and backups available but cannot promise it will always be uninterrupted. Always check critical bookings and times with the supplier. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the amount you paid for Plus in the 12 months before a claim.

Changes

If we change prices or these terms, we will tell you in the app at least 30 days before the change affects you. You can cancel before it takes effect.
