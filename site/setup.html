1. Install it on your phone

Installing puts Guidewise on your home screen and makes it work with no signal.

Android (Samsung, Pixel and others)

  1. Open guidewise-app.netlify.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right). If Desktop site is ticked, untick it.
  3. Tap ⋮ again, then Install app (or Add to home screen, then Install).

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open guidewise-app.netlify.app in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open guidewise-app.netlify.app.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.
Only "Add shortcut", or the app looks tiny like a website?
  1. Make sure you opened the link in Chrome itself, not inside WhatsApp or Gmail. If unsure, tap ⋮, then Open in Chrome.
  2. Turn off Desktop site in the ⋮ menu.
  3. If Guidewise is already on your phone, uninstall it first, then install again.

2. Your first tour

Briefing has a Send on WhatsApp button: it opens WhatsApp with the message ready, you pick the group and tap send.

The bottom bar has five places: Today, Day, Guests, Briefing and More.

  1. Today shows the day that matters now (today, or your next tour day), what is on now and next, and your next steps: what to pack, the briefing to send, birthdays, dietary needs to pass on.
  2. Day: your timeline. Tap Edit day to set the date, meeting point, departure time and stops with drive and stay minutes. All times move together. Tap Done when finished.
  3. Guests: tap a guest to edit rooms, birthdays, dietary needs and notes. Dietaries show on every lunch stop. Birthdays alert you on the day.
  4. Briefing: tomorrow's WhatsApp message, ready to copy. It never includes birthdays or dietaries.
  5. More: checklist, documents (readable with no signal), local intel, AI import, all tours and backup.

On tour

  • Call and Directions: in Edit day, add a venue's phone and address. Each stop then has 📞 Call and 🧭 Directions buttons, and Guidewise remembers the venue for your next tour.
  • Weather, sun times and holidays: add the town or area for each day in Edit day. Sunrise and sunset show with the forecast, and public holidays in that country become a next step and appear in Local intel. The forecast appears on Today and goes into the briefing by itself, up to 15 days ahead. Strong wind or rain shows as a next step.
  • Briefing in another language (Plus): on Briefing, pick a language under "Also in another language" and tap ✦ Translate. Send the translation, or both versions in one message.
  • Dietaries to the venue: on a lunch stop, tap 🍽 Send dietaries. A WhatsApp message with guest numbers, time and each dietary need opens, addressed to the venue if you saved its number.
  • Plan to your driver: at the bottom of the day plan, tap 🚐 Send plan to driver. Times, stops, addresses and venue numbers, with no guest details.
  • Departure reminders: on a tour day, tap 🔕 Reminders off on Today to switch them on. Guidewise buzzes 10 minutes before you leave each stop while it is open, and shows a notification if you allow it. Phones may pause this when Guidewise is closed.
  • Running late: tap ⏱ Running late on Today or ⏱ Late on the day plan. Choose how many minutes and from which stop. Every later stop moves, and a WhatsApp update with the new times is ready to send. Undo puts it back.
  • Cash and tips: under More (or the tile on Today). Log the float you received, entrance fees, meals, tips you paid and transport, and see your balance. Tips you receive are kept separate. Send the summary to your operator by WhatsApp.
  • End-of-day report: under More, or on Today in the evening. Add notes for your operator; the programme, any running-late changes and the day's cash are filled in. Send by WhatsApp or email.
  • Venues: under More. Every venue you saved a phone or address for, with notes. Type a saved name into a stop and its details fill in.
  • Day templates: on a day you run often, tap ☰ Templates, then Save. On any tour, use it on a day or add it as a new day: stops, times, venues and checklist come with it.
  • Receipt photos: when you add spending under Cash and tips, tap 📷 Add receipt photo. Share all receipts with your operator in one go.
  • Print or PDF: under More. The whole tour or one day, with the rooming list and dietaries if you want them. Choose Save as PDF in the print window to send it.
  • Headcount: on a tour day, tap Headcount on Today (or under More) and tap each guest back on board. It shows who is still missing.

A sample tour opens first. Tap its name at the top to switch or rename it, or start your own from Today.

Several tours

Tap the tour name at the top (with the ▾) to see all your tours and switch between them, start a + New tour, import one, or rename the open tour. Tap Manage tours there to Duplicate to reuse an itinerary (days and checklists are copied, guests are not), and Archive to tidy away finished tours. Documents belong to the tour you add them to.

Everything stays on your phone unless you switch on backup.

3. Switch on backup (recommended)

So a lost or broken phone does not cost you your planning. Your tours and documents (vouchers, PDFs, photos) are both backed up, encrypted.

  1. Tap Back up at the top right of the app.
  2. Enter your email and tap Email me a sign-in code. Type the 6-digit code from the email. Check spam if it does not arrive.
  3. Read and tick the three boxes, then I agree, continue. What the boxes mean
  4. Choose a passphrase of 8 or more characters, for example two or three words. Save it in your password manager.
  5. You then get a recovery code. Screenshot it or write it down, and keep it away from your phone. Tap I have saved it.
Your tour is locked on your phone before upload. Nobody else can read it, including us. Your passphrase or your recovery code is the only way in, so keep both safe.

What the three boxes mean

1. "My guests know I keep their details…"

South Africa's privacy law, POPIA, makes you responsible for the guest details you record. Dietary needs, allergies and mobility notes count as health information, so guests must agree to them being written down.

In practice: tell guests at your welcome briefing that you note dietary and medical needs to pass on to restaurants and hotels, and record only what you need. Most operators' booking forms already ask for this.

2. "…stored with Supabase in London, UK"

Your backup has to live somewhere other than your phone. Guidewise uses Supabase, a well-known data storage company, and keeps the backup on their computers in London.

London is outside South Africa. POPIA (section 72) only allows personal information to go abroad under certain conditions: the UK's strong privacy law is one, and your agreement is another. This box records your agreement, so both are covered.

Before anything leaves your phone it is locked with your passphrase. Supabase only ever holds scrambled data that neither they nor we can read.

3. "Guidewise cannot read or recover my backup…"

Because only your passphrase or recovery code can unlock the backup, nobody can reset it for you. Keep both safe. If you lose both, the cloud copy is lost, but the tour on your phone is not affected.

On a new phone

  1. Install Guidewise, tap Back up and sign in with your email code.
  2. Enter your passphrase (or tap Use recovery code), then Restore backup.
  3. Documents come back with the restore. On a phone that already has your tours, tap Get documents from cloud under Back up.

4. AI import (Plus)

Turn an itinerary, rooming list or recap into a ready tour in about a minute. 5 imports a month.

  1. Sign in under Back up first (your 30-day trial includes it).
  2. Tap the tour name at the top, then ✦ Import. Or on the Docs tab, tap Build the tour from documents.
  3. Tap Choose documents, or tap a document you already saved in the tour. Up to 5: PDF, Excel (.xlsx), CSV, text or a photo. Old .xls or Word files: save them as PDF first.
  4. Tick the box to agree, then Read my documents. Keep Guidewise open while it reads.
  5. Check the draft, choose New tour or add it to the open tour, then tap Create tour.
  6. Go through each day. Items marked ⚠ Check were unclear in the documents.
Spreadsheets are cleaned on your phone before sending: passport, flight, phone, email and address columns are removed and dates of birth become birthdays. Only send documents your operator allows you to share.

Help

I do not see the latest version

Close Guidewise completely and open it again.

No sign-in email

Check spam, and check the email address for typos. Wait a minute before asking for another code.

Forgot my passphrase

On the unlock screen tap Use recovery code. If you have lost both, tap Start a new backup: the tour on your phone is kept.
